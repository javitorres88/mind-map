## 🩺 Theme 1 — Chronic Conditions

- **What are they?**
  - Persist ≥ 1 year and require ongoing medical attention
  - Usually *managed*, not cured
  - Often coexist with other conditions (comorbidity)

- **Common conditions**
  - Cardiovascular — hypertension, heart disease, stroke
  - Metabolic — diabetes (type 1 / type 2), obesity
  - Respiratory — asthma, COPD
  - Musculoskeletal — arthritis, osteoporosis, back pain
  - Neurological — Alzheimer’s, Parkinson’s, epilepsy, MS
  - Mental health — depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder
  - Kidney & liver — chronic kidney disease, fatty liver
  - Autoimmune — rheumatoid arthritis, lupus, IBD, psoriasis

- **Risk factors**
  - Tobacco use, poor diet, physical inactivity, excess alcohol
  - Aging population
  - Genetics & family history
  - Environmental exposure

- **Burden & impact**
  - Leading cause of death and disability worldwide
  - ~6 in 10 U.S. adults have ≥1 chronic condition; 4 in 10 have 2+
  - Major driver of healthcare spending
  - Reduced quality of life

- **Management & care**
  - Medication adherence
  - Lifestyle modification — diet, exercise, sleep
  - Regular monitoring & check-ups
  - Multidisciplinary care teams
  - Patient education & self-management

- **Challenges**
  - Cost & access to care
  - Fragmented care
  - Adherence & follow-through
  - Health inequities

## 🤖 Theme 2 — Artificial Intelligence

- **What is AI?**
  - Machines that simulate human intelligence
  - Learns from data and improves over time

- **Core subfields**
  - Machine learning — supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement
  - Deep learning & neural networks
  - Natural language processing & large language models (transformers, GPT)
  - Computer vision
  - Generative AI
  - Robotics & automation

- **Key technologies**
  - Large language models
  - Predictive analytics
  - Pattern recognition
  - IoT & wearable sensors
  - Cloud & edge computing

- **AI in healthcare**
  - Medical imaging & diagnostics
  - Clinical decision support
  - Drug discovery & development
  - Personalized treatment plans
  - Virtual assistants & chatbots
  - Administrative automation

- **Data**
  - Electronic health records (EHR)
  - Wearable & sensor data
  - Genomics & other omics
  - Clinical trials & research data
  - Synthetic / training data

- **Challenges & ethics**
  - Bias & fairness
  - Privacy & data security
  - Explainability (the "black box" problem)
  - Regulation & compliance
  - Trust & accountability

## ✚ Where the two themes meet — AI applied to chronic conditions

- AI-assisted early detection & diagnosis of chronic disease
- Predictive risk stratification from EHR & wearable data
- Personalized treatment & care plans
- Remote patient monitoring & anomaly alerts
- Medication adherence coaching & reminders
- Virtual health assistants & mental-health chatbots
- Clinical decision support for care teams
- Population health analytics & disease surveillance
